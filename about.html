ABOUT AERIS / AIR INTELLIGENCE

Understand
the air.
See what matters.

AERIS is a smart air quality monitoring system designed to make environmental data accessible, understandable, and meaningful. By combining sensor technology, connected data infrastructure, and real-time visualisation, AERIS brings essential environmental readings into one unified interface, helping users better understand the air around them and make informed decisions.

01 / THE SYSTEM

PURPOSE & APPROACH

Making environmental information easier to understand.

Environmental conditions can change throughout the day, and understanding those changes starts with having access to useful information. AERIS brings air quality measurements and environmental readings together in a clear monitoring interface.

Built around connected sensors and a cloud-backed data system, AERIS is designed to make collected readings easier to access, interpret, and monitor over time.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

FROM MEASUREMENT TO INSIGHT

Connected technology. Clearer information.

AERIS uses environmental sensors connected to an ESP32-based monitoring device to collect readings such as particulate matter, temperature, humidity, and gas sensor values. The device communicates available data to Firebase Firestore, where the connected dashboard can retrieve and display it.

This approach brings the sensing hardware and digital monitoring interface into one connected system, making environmental readings easier to review in a single place.

03 / VISIBILITY

See more than a number.

Individual sensor readings become more useful when presented with context. AERIS organises available measurements into readable values and visual indicators to help users understand monitored conditions.

04 / ACCESSIBILITY

Environmental data, within reach.

A connected dashboard provides a central place to review device readings and environmental conditions. AERIS aims to make monitoring straightforward without requiring users to interpret raw sensor output on their own.

05 / TECHNOLOGY

THE FOUNDATION

Hardware meets connected monitoring.

HARDWARE

ESP32 & environmental sensors

The device combines an ESP32 with sensors for particulate matter, temperature, humidity, and gas-related readings.

DATA INFRASTRUCTURE

Firebase Firestore

A cloud-hosted data layer connects the monitoring device with the dashboard and stores available device readings.

INTERFACE

A unified monitoring dashboard

A web-based interface presents collected readings, device information, and environmental indicators in an organised format.

AQI

ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS

Better information starts with better visibility.

Air quality monitoring can help people become more aware of environmental conditions and changes in the air around them. AERIS presents collected measurements to support awareness and informed interpretation. Sensor readings are intended for monitoring purposes and should not be treated as a substitute for certified reference measurements or medical advice.
