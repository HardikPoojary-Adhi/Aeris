AERIS — Smart Air Quality Index & Monitoring System (SAQIMS) — is an Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) project designed to make environmental monitoring more accessible and understandable. Using connected sensors, an ESP32-based monitoring device, and a cloud-connected dashboard, AERIS collects and presents environmental readings through a unified interface, helping users observe air quality conditions and interpret available data.

01 / THE SYSTEM

PURPOSE & APPROACH

Making environmental information easier to understand.

Environmental conditions can change throughout the day, and understanding those changes starts with having access to useful information. AERIS brings air quality measurements and environmental readings together in a clear monitoring interface.

SAQIMS is built around connected sensing hardware and a cloud-backed data system. It is designed to make collected readings easier to access, interpret, and monitor through a central dashboard.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

FROM MEASUREMENT TO INSIGHT

Connected technology. Clearer information.

AERIS uses an ESP32-based monitoring device connected to environmental sensors. The hardware includes an SDS011 particulate matter sensor, a DHT22 temperature and humidity sensor, and MQ-series gas sensors.

The device sends available readings to Firebase Firestore, where the connected web dashboard can retrieve and display the data. This links physical sensor measurements with a digital monitoring interface.

03 / VISIBILITY

See more than a number.

Individual sensor readings become more useful when presented clearly. AERIS organises available measurements into readable values and visual indicators to help users understand monitored environmental conditions.

04 / ACCESSIBILITY

Environmental data, within reach.

A connected web dashboard provides a central place to review device readings and environmental conditions. The goal is to make monitoring straightforward without requiring users to interpret raw sensor output on their own.

05 / TECHNOLOGY

THE FOUNDATION

Hardware meets connected monitoring.

HARDWARE

ESP32 & environmental sensors

The monitoring device combines an ESP32 with sensors for particulate matter, temperature, humidity, and gas-related measurements.

DATA INFRASTRUCTURE

Firebase Firestore

The cloud-hosted database connects the monitoring device with the dashboard and stores available device readings.

INTERFACE

A unified monitoring dashboard

The web interface presents collected readings, device information, and environmental indicators in an organised format.

07 / AIR QUALITY

ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS

Better information starts with better visibility.

Air quality monitoring can help people become more aware of environmental conditions and changes in the air around them. AERIS presents collected measurements to support awareness and informed interpretation.

Sensor readings are intended for monitoring and educational purposes. They should not be treated as a substitute for certified reference measurements, official air quality advisories, or medical advice.
